Before you book

Questions people actually ask

Is $9.90 the price of the scrub or the spa?

No. $9.90 is our fee for getting you a confirmed booking, in English, with the paperwork. The treatment is paid to the venue on the day, at their published price. We take no cut from the spa.

Do I need a Korean phone number or a Korean card?

No. That’s the whole point — the venue’s booking page asks for both, and we’re the ones who get past it. You need WhatsApp or email, and PayPal for the $9.90.

I have tattoos. Can I still go?

Many Korean bathhouses and scrub rooms restrict tattoos, and it varies by venue. We ask the specific venue before booking and tell you the real answer — including “no”. We never promise access we haven’t confirmed.

Will I be naked? Who is in the room?

It depends on the venue: some scrub rooms are one-person and private, others are on a shared floor, and some provide disposable underwear. We ask about the room type, what you wear, and whether the therapist is the same sex as you, and we put their answer in the voucher. We never guess.

Can I choose how hard the scrub is?

Yes — soft, medium or “시원하게” (strong). You pick it on the form, it goes on the therapist’s card in Korean, and you can still change your mind on the day.

What if I’m on my period, pregnant, or have allergies?

Tell us on the form. Many venues ask guests to use a tampon or menstrual cup, and some decline guests who are menstruating. We confirm before booking so you aren’t turned away at the counter. Allergies and pregnancy go to the therapist in Korean.

Do you come with me?

No. We don’t escort or interpret in person — you go alone with the voucher and the Korean card, and the staff read it in one second. If something goes wrong on site, message us and we call the venue for you.

How fast is it, really?

You hear from us within 2 hours between 09:00 and 22:00 KST, seven days a week. The confirmed voucher comes in 2–4 hours, and it needs all three of your time options. Requests sent overnight are answered by 11:00.

What if the venue is fully booked?

You get 100% of the $9.90 back the same day, or we keep trying another venue if you’d rather. There’s no “we’ll see what we can do” middle state.

Which areas and which places?

Seoul — Hongdae, Yeonnam, Hapjeong, Seongsu, Gangnam, plus brand spas such as SPA 1978 in Daechi. If you already have a venue in mind, send the Instagram or Naver Map link and we check that exact branch. If you don’t, we suggest two or three that fit your budget and area. For hair and scalp work we usually start with MY SPRING, HAIR in Sinsa (private 1:1, English spoken, closed Thu & Sun) — you can simply pick “Not sure yet” on the form.

Is this a spa? Do you guarantee results?

We’re a booking desk, not a spa, a clinic or a medical service. We describe what happens at the venue, in their words — no detox claims, no “cures”, no before-and-after promises. If you want medical treatment, this is the wrong service and we’d rather say so.

What do you do with my details?

Your name as on your passport, country, WhatsApp, email — plus the answers we need for the venue. Never your passport number, ID number, or card details. We share your name, party size and contact with that one venue for that one booking, and we delete the request and messages 90 days after the voucher is issued.
