Where it stops

The four places it stops

① The channel is Korean

전화 · 카카오톡 · DM
  • Phone — in Korean. During treatments nobody is at the desk to pick up.
  • KakaoTalk — the messenger works with a foreign number, so you can send a message. Replies assume Korean, and anything involving identity verification or Kakao's payment rails is built for a Korean line and a Korean bank account.
  • Instagram DM — the fastest route when it works. But a one-person shop is, by definition, one person: messages sit unread for days.

② Deposits and payment

예약금 · 해외카드
  • Some shops take a ₩10,000–20,000 deposit at booking, usually deducted from the final bill. If the account is a Korean bank, you cannot send it from abroad.
  • Some shops do not accept overseas cards — often not stated anywhere until you are standing at the counter.
  • Listed prices sometimes exclude VAT.

③ One person has no spare hands

1인 운영
  • A one-chair salon cannot answer the phone mid-treatment.
  • Enquiries sent during a treatment roll over to the next day.
  • “No reply” usually means “no hands”, not “not interested” — which is exactly why giving up there wastes a good find.

④ Menu and price mismatches

60분의 의미가 다르다
  • “60-minute head spa” means different things: scalp-camera diagnosis and scaling, or a careful wash at the basin; a shared floor, or a private room.
  • Long hair (below the shoulder) commonly carries a ₩10,000–20,000 surcharge.
  • Settle this before the appointment, not at the counter.
Before you pay

The 12-point pre-booking checklist

Fill this in before you build your day around a treatment. Items you cannot fill in are usually the ones that turn into trouble on the day.

The shop

1–3
  • Is it still open? Check the date of the newest Instagram post and the newest Google review — not the follower count.
  • Is this the right branch and the right floor? Same-name branches and stacked floors in one building are common.
  • Is it a salon or a clinic? Head spa in Korea is a beauty service — scalp cleansing, scaling, massage, wash and dry. It is not a medical treatment and it is not sold as a cure.

Booking and money

4–8
  • Which channel does it actually use — phone, Kakao, DM, a form, its own booking page?
  • Is a deposit required, and how much?
  • Can you pay by overseas card, or is it local card/cash only?
  • Cancellation deadline. Next-day-and-later is often non-refundable; same-day no-shows often forfeit the lot.
  • What happens if you are late? Ten minutes can mean cancellation with the deposit kept.

The treatment

9–12
  • Final price: long-hair surcharge, VAT included or not.
  • Total time including arrival, consultation and payment. Leave two hours.
  • Pressure: soft, medium or strong — say it before you lie down, especially for a Korean scrub.
  • Anything the therapist must know: pregnancy, sensitive scalp, alcohol allergy, tattoos (some bathhouse-style venues restrict entry), menstruation for scrub bookings.

One line saves a trip

Ask, in writing, for the final amount before you start — long-hair surcharge and VAT included. A message that asks for a number gets a number; a message that asks for a menu gets a photo of the menu.

On the day

The flow, and the things that make it easy

  1. Arrive 5–10 minutes earlyThe appointment time is usually when the consultation begins.
  2. Consultation firstScalp camera, then the course. This is the moment to lock the final price.
  3. The treatmentMost Korean head spas wash at a basin, so you leave with wet hair; drying is included. Book it in the morning rather than an hour before dinner.
  4. No tippingThe listed price is the whole price.
  5. No-shows are taken seriouslyDeposits are kept and repeat offenders get refused. If you cannot make it, message as early as you can.
Timing. One-chair salons fill first on weekend afternoons. If a weekend is your only window, book Friday evening or Saturday morning rather than Saturday afternoon. Weekday mornings are the quietest slots in Seoul, and some shops price them lower.
Bring some won in cash. It solves the declined-overseas-card problem on the spot, and it lets you offer to pay a deposit in person when the shop asks for a transfer.
