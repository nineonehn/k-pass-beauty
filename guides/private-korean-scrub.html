How to book a private Korean body scrub in Seoul

A private Korean body scrub (sesin) is not the same as a public bathhouse admission. If privacy matters, confirm the exact room arrangement before paying.

Ask about privacy

Request a one-person room if that is what you want. Ask what clothing is expected, who will be in the room, and what therapist arrangements the venue can confirm.

Check venue policies

Ask directly about tattoos, cancellation, deposits, treatment duration and pressure preferences. Rules vary by venue; avoid relying on general travel advice.

Book the exact branch

Use the venue’s own reservation channel if available. If you cannot communicate with it, send K-Pass Beauty the link and your preferred times for booking assistance.

Need booking help?

The early-bird booking fee is US$9.90 by card (no PayPal account needed) or PayPal. You pay the venue separately for your treatment. If none of your three proposed times can be confirmed, the booking fee is refunded in full.
